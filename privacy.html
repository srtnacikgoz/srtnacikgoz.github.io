Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Kanal Motoru ("the app") is a personal tool used by its operator to publish videos to YouTube channels the operator owns. The app is not available to the public.

Google user data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload and manage access: to upload videos, thumbnails and captions and to set titles, descriptions and end screens on the operator's own channels.
  • YouTube and YouTube Analytics read access: to read the operator's own channel and video details and statistics.
  • Authorization tokens: the access and refresh tokens Google issues when the operator connects a channel.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the operator's own videos and to show the operator statistics about their own channels. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with any third party.

How the data is stored

Authorization tokens and channel statistics are stored in a private, access-controlled database used only by this app. Tokens are kept only as long as the channel is connected.

Deleting your data or revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Once revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
