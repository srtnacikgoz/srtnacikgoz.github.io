Kanal Motoru

A private publishing tool for a small set of YouTube channels.

Kanal Motoru is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by a single person. It prepares long-form videos (narration, images and music) and uploads them to YouTube channels that the operator owns and manages.

The tool is not offered to the public. There is no sign-up, and no one other than the operator connects their Google account to it.

What it does with Google

  • Uploads finished videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions to the operator's own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads basic statistics (views, watch time) for those same channels using the YouTube Analytics API.
